This folder is NOT openly licensed. Every line and transformer impedance and rating in it comes from
the JAO Core Static Grid Model (9th release, March 2026), RTE's rows. JAO's General Terms and
Conditions allow educational, academic, research and internal-analysis use with JAO cited as the

written authorisation (contact@jao.eu; https://www.jao.eu/static-grid-model). Keep this case inside the build pipeline.

UnvalidatedAtScale: this case has NOT been validated against official studies. It is a solvable
approximation of the French 225/400 kV grid for one snapshot (2026-01-06 10:00 CET, the winter
2025-26 consumption peak), with allocated demand and register-based generation. Do not use it for
planning, operation or investment decisions.
