# How this model was built: Japan east 50 Hz transmission 500/275/220/154/66 kV winter evening 2025-01-15 18:00 JST (UnvalidatedAtScale)

These models were generated by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), working with full access to GridSim's core code base. That access is what allowed generic but detailed models to exist and to be grounded in real, published data from real operators around the world. These models are not approved for anything other than basic demonstrations. They do provide an exceptional starting point for people to expand into a much more detailed system.

General-purpose fidelity only. This is a solvable approximation for one snapshot, never planning grade, and it has not been validated against official studies (hence UnvalidatedAtScale in its name). It was assembled by a private build pipeline using GridSim's solver, from published open data only, and checked with GridSim's own power flow.

## 3. How it was constructed and iterated

Method in brief: a snapshot build. The data the map needs was fetched once; the network was assembled (voltage classes, cuts at the borders, line and cable parameters from standard types, transformer units, plant placement, demand allocation, dispatch, slack); then solved with GridSim. Each round let the solver's findings (reactive shortfalls, overloads, angle spread, islands, fault levels) point at the next data gap. Rules held throughout: no value is changed just because it clears a solver symptom (a value adjusted to solve with no source is tagged synthetic and listed below); every value carries an evidence class (attested, derived, estimated, synthetic); a deeper, better-sourced round is kept even where it looks worse, because it exposes a real data gap; no round was ever overwritten.

### Round notes (before / after numbers as recorded at each round; some rows cover sister grids built in the same batch)

#### Refinement rounds (2026-11-01): east r4, west r3, east spine r2

All three use the PATCHED topology variant v4 (a build step, then a build step and
a build step). The shared adapter's own output in `model/<grid>/` is not changed, and
a build step is neither edited nor re-run. The patch writes `model/<grid>_v4/` and applies only OSM topology facts:

- P1: a line takes the circuit count of its own OSM way when that way covers at least half the route.
  The adapter keeps the smallest count along a chain (Towada 500 kV 114 km came out as 1 circuit). This changed
  81 east and 74 west lines.
- P2: lines that end at a plant outline (no mapped substation) were dropped by the adapter. They are
  restored with a 'plant switchyard' bus: 54 lines in all, including Reihoku 500 kV and Joetsu's 60 Hz 275 kV line.
- Register: GPPD and OSM plants are matched by name or capacity within 10 km (r1-r3 counted Anegasaki,
  Ohgishima and Yokohama twice). A matched GPPD row moves to the OSM outline.
- Placement: a plant goes first to its own switchyard (the P2 bus, else a bus of its class within 1.5 km).
 Joetsu (2,380 MW, Chubu / JERA) is now in the WEST case.
- East now has the two-bank coupling floor (build.TX_BANKS), so the code and the east case agree.

Still open: the Tokyo bay 275 kV cables are not in OSM, and TEPCO PG's capacity PDFs refuse automated fetch (HTTP
403). 11.7 GW of plant therefore lands on Minamikawasaki 275 kV, and the export cap moves 6.7 GW of it elsewhere. The
66 kV chains between 275 kV islands (Ikegami - Wadahori) still carry the angle difference. Unplaced plants at full
depth are now 34 rows, 6,922.6 MW, all on isolated grids (Hokkaido, Okinawa, Sado, Iki) apart from one 19 MW hydro.

## 5. Known gaps

### Known gaps

- Line impedances and ratings are TYPICAL per-km values by voltage and construction, not the utilities' own [VERIFY]; circuit counts are OSM's tags (1 when untagged, so double-circuit routes drawn once are under-counted).
- Transformers are synthetic (one coupling per voltage pair per site, only where OSM gives evidence: a voltage tag on the site or a mapped transformer); taps and OLTCs are not modelled.
- No reactive compensation is mapped; the bus shunts are fixed repairs (synthetic).
- Plant register: WRI GPPD v1.3 (2021) placed by nearness; 34 rows (6922.6 MW, incl. Hokkaido and Okinawa) have no bus in reach and are left out; GPPD lists some units retired since 2021 and misses newer ones [VERIFY].
- Dispatch: each source's attested area output pooled over the grid and split pro rata to placed capacity; nuclear only at the stations operating in January 2025; solar, wind, biomass, batteries and 'other' netted off area demand as embedded generation.
- Demand: attested area demand x 0.97 (losses assumed 3 %) minus embedded generation, spread by GeoNames population to the nearest bus of 154 kV or below; power factor 0.98 (assumed).
- HVDC and frequency converters are fixed injections; flows from the area interchange balance split by link rating ([VERIFY] against OCCTO tie-line flow records).
