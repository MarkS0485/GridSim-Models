planning reports). It is a solvable rough map of the NEM mainland (QLD, NSW + ACT, VIC, SA) for one snapshot (2025-07-01 18:00 AEST (UTC+10; the 5-minute interval ending 18:00), the highest mainland NEM operational demand of June-August 2025 (AEMO price-and-demand files)): topology
