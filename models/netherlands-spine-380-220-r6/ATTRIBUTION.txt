JAO SOURCE TERMS
----------------
Every TenneT line and transformer impedance and rating in it
comes from the JAO Core Static Grid Model (9th release, March 2026). JAO's General Terms and
Conditions allow educational, academic, research and internal-analysis use with JAO cited as the
source; selling, commercialising or making the data available to third parties requires JAO's
prior written authorisation (contact@jao.eu; https://www.jao.eu/static-grid-model).

UnvalidatedAtScale: this case has NOT been validated against official studies. It is a solvable
approximation of the Dutch 220/380 kV grid for one snapshot (2025-11-19 17:30 CET), with allocated
demand and register-based generation. Do not use it for planning, operation or investment decisions.
