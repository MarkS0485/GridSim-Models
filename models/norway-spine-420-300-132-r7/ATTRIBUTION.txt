UnvalidatedAtScale: this case has NOT been validated against official studies. It is a solvable
approximation of the Norwegian 420 / 300 / 132 kV grid for one snapshot (2026-01-07 16:30 CET, the
2025-26 winter peak quarter-hour), with connectivity recovered from line geometry, standard per-km
line parameters, register-based generation and population-allocated demand. Do not use it for
planning, operation or investment decisions.
