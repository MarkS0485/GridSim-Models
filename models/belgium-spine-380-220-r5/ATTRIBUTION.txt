UnvalidatedAtScale: this case has NOT been validated against official studies. It is a solvable
approximation of the Belgium 220 kV-and-above grid for one snapshot (2025-11-19 17:30 CET), with
allocated demand and register-based generation. Do not use it for planning, operation or investment
decisions.
